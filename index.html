Retypesetting engine · four layers

A scan goes in. Real, selectable type comes out.

Typecraft reads a flat page the way a typesetter would: background fills, rules and table borders, styled text, and artwork. Then it rebuilds the page from scratch as a vector PDF — every word redrawn as Helvetica at the original size, colour and weight, not pasted back in as a picture. Nothing leaves this tab.
